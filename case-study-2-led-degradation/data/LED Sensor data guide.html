What does an
AXON EQ report?

Two telemetry streams. One AXON EQ inset light node emits 45 operational metrics covering alarms, connectivity and wave depth — plus a separate IoT sensor stream of 47 metrics covering temperature, motion, environment and power. This taxonomy maps what each value means and why it matters.
